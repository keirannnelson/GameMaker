How to Use Game Maker



Game Maker uses machine learning and statistical analysis to predict the winning team and highlight the most impactful players in upcoming games. Whether you’re a fan, analyst, or fantasy league player, our platform helps you make smarter predictions and better understand the game.


Predictions


Visit the Predict page, select your dates, choose your games, and get predictions powered by machine learning! First you have to select your dates. In the top left there are two fields 'Start Date' and 'End Date', here you can input the dates which you want to query. Once you've selected your dates then click 'RETRIEVE' to view the games. Shown will be all matchups within the date range you selected, along with each team's win-loss. Now select which games you want to predict on using the checkbox in the leftmost column of the tables. When you've selected all the desired games click predict to see the model's outputs.



Matchups


Visit the Matchups page, select your date, choose your teams, and get predictions powered by machine learning! First you have to select your date. In the 'Select Date' field, input the dates which you want to query. Then, drag and drop your two chosen teams to the basketball court. Once you've selected your dates then click 'RETRIEVE' to view the games. Shown will be all matchups between the two teams on and after the date you selected, along with each team's win-loss. Now, click the 'PREDICT' button to see the model's outputs.
